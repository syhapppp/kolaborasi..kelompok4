Dari Sisa Jadi Istimewa

Belajar untuk mengolah sampah menjadi produk yang layak guna dengan menggunakan prinsip 3R (Reuse,Recycle,Reduce).Proyek ini menggabungkan beberapa mata pelajaran dengan menggunakan metode SDLC waterfall.

Permasalahan yang Dialami

Limbah motherboard merupakan salah satu jenis sampah elektronik yang dapat menimbulkan berbagai permasalahan bagi lingkungan. Motherboard bekas mengandung berbagai bahan seperti logam, plastik, dan zat kimia yang berpotensi mencemari tanah serta air apabila dibuang sembarangan. Selain itu, komponen motherboard sulit terurai secara alami sehingga dapat menyebabkan penumpukan sampah elektronik. Jika tidak dikelola dengan baik, limbah tersebut juga dapat membahayakan kesehatan manusia akibat paparan zat berbahaya yang terkandung di dalamnya. Oleh karena itu, diperlukan upaya kreatif untuk memanfaatkan kembali limbah motherboard menjadi barang yang memiliki nilai guna dan nilai estetika.

Penyelesaian Masalah

Limbah motherboard dapat mencemari lingkungan jika tidak dikelola dengan baik. Oleh karena itu, diperlukan solusi dengan mendaur ulang motherboard bekas menjadi kerajinan yang bermanfaat dan bernilai jual, seperti lampu hias.

Explanation Text

1 Aulladina Syafa Febriana

Electronic waste is one of the environmental problems caused by the rapid development of technology. Many computer components, such as motherboards, are discarded when they are damaged or no longer needed. However, these components can still be reused to create something useful and beautiful. One creative way to reduce electronic waste is by transforming an old motherboard into a decorative lamp. This transformation shows how unused electronic materials can become an attractive and valuable product.

The transformation begins with preparing an old motherboard, wires, LED lights, and other supporting materials. The wires are arranged and shaped to create a small decorative tree with several twigs. Colorful LED lights are added to make the tree look brighter and more attractive. The motherboard is then prepared to serve as the base of the lamp. Each component is carefully arranged and connected to create a beautiful decoration.

After all the parts are prepared, the decorative tree is placed on the motherboard, which serves as the base or stand of the lamp. The wires are arranged into tree-shaped twigs, with colorful LED lights at their ends. The motherboard provides a sturdy base and makes the decoration look more unique. After the adapter is connected, the lights can shine beautifully. Finally, the materials become a creative and attractive decorative lamp.

As a result, an old motherboard is transformed into a unique decorative lamp stand with beautiful glowing twigs. The colorful LED lights make the product look more attractive and brighten up the room. Besides being a creative decoration, this product gives unused electronic components a new purpose. Therefore, this project helps reduce electronic waste and encourages people to recycle old materials into useful products.

Explanation Text

2 Nasywa Anindya Salma

Electronic waste is an environmental issue stemming from rapid technological advancement. Motherboards are one such type of electronic waste. If discarded improperly, this waste can have harmful effects on both humans and the environment. To prevent such negative impacts, we can recycle this waste into useful items—for instance, by transforming it into a decorative lamp shaped like a tree.

The recycling process begins by selecting discarded, unused motherboards and cleaning them to remove any dust or debris. Components that protrude significantly from the motherboard should be removed to save space. At this stage, the motherboard—typically used as the primary circuit board for a computer—is repurposed into a decorative element with artistic value.

The cleaned motherboard is combined with wire shaped like a tree, with colorful LED lights attached to serve as leaves. The LEDs are positioned to produce a beautiful, multicolored glow. This combination creates an attractive and striking appearance. Once all components and electrical circuits are properly installed, the upcycled product is ready for use.

It is far better to recycle a damaged computer motherboard and transform it into something useful rather than discarding it carelessly, which can have harmful effects on both humans and the environment. This product serves not only as room decor—as a decorative lamp—but also helps reduce the accumulation of electronic waste. Therefore, repurposing waste into a functional item is a tangible step that significantly contributes to creating a safe and clean environment.

This project serves as a way to reduce electronic waste, which can be harmful to the environment. As a result, discarded electronic components are transformed into attractive "electronic trees." These products serve a decorative purpose and can be displayed as creative ornaments. Furthermore, this project fosters creativity, collaboration, and environmental awareness. Therefore, recycling electronic waste into electronic trees is a meaningful way to reduce waste and support a cleaner environment.
